Admissions

Now open for A.Y. 2026-2027!


At O.B. Montessori Center, we are ready to help you with a smooth admission and enrollment experience for you and your child.


APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS


Junior Casa and Junior Advanced Casa (Pre-Kindergarten 1 and 2)


    - Junior Casa corresponds to Pre-Kindergarten 1. The applicant must be three (3) years old by August 31 of the next academic year. The child is required to undergo a one-week trial.
    - Junior Advanced Casa corresponds to Pre-Kindergarten 2. The applicant must be four (4) years old by August 31 of the next academic year.

For Filipino Applicants


    - A scanned (PDF) copy of the Birth Certificate authenticated by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
    - A scanned (PDF) copy of a recent photo, passport size (4.5 cm. x 3.5 cm. or 1.8 in. x 1.4 in.)
    - A scanned (PDF) copy Report Card (SF9) (if applicable)

For Foreign Applicants


(In addition to the above requirements)
    - A scanned (PDF) copy of the Birth Certificate (with English translation as applicable)
    - A scanned (PDF) copy of the Passport with Biodata page and Visa Status
    - A scanned (PDF) copy of the Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR) I-Card issued by the Bureau of Immigration
    - Documentary Requirements for Special Study Permit—
    https://immigration.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/pdf/SpecialPermits/SSP_Rev2.pdf

    https://immigration.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/pdf/SpecialPermits/CGAF_003_Rev2_2016Jul01.pdf

For Applicants with Dual Citizenship


(In addition to the above requirements)
    - A scanned (PDF) copy of a valid Philippine Passport
    - Certificate of Recognition as a Filipino Citizen or Identification Certificate as a Filipino

Grade 1


    - Applicants must be six (6) years old by August 31 of the next academic year.
    - The applicant must have completed kindergarten level from a Department of Education accredited school and issued a student’s Learner Reference Number (LRN).

Grades 2-5


    - Applicants must be seven (7) to ten (10) years old.
    - The applicant must have a quarterly general average of at least 80% with no grade lower than 78% in any academic subjects and in deportment.
    - Applicants with grades below the minimum requirements are welcome to apply. However, the application is subject to the approval of the Admissions Committee.
